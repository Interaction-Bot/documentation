# Features

The **Features** menu of the website (`interaction-bot.com/features`) lists every feature of the bot, grouped by category: **Translation**, **Welcome & onboarding**, **Voice**, **Moderation & safety** and **Support & automation**. No login is needed.

Each feature has its own public page, with a short description, how to start in three steps, the commands to use, the **limits by plan** (Free and Premium, see [Premium](../premium/premium.md)), and a button to configure it on the [dashboard](dashboard/README.md).
