# FAQ

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dash-faq.png" alt="FAQ page"><figcaption><p>FAQ page</p></figcaption></figure>

## Localization

Navbar > category _**Audio & AI**_ > _**FAQ**_. Members use it with [`/ask`](../../commands/faq.md).

## How it works

Add questions and answers to a group, then choose the channels where the bot uses them. The bot answers in the language of the question, with the answer you wrote. With auto reply on, the bot answers by itself when it is sure enough of an answer.

## Create a group

Click _**Create group**_, then:

* **Name** (up to 40 characters, unique) and the **Enabled** toggle.
* **Channels**: the channels where the bot uses this group. A channel belongs to one group only.
* **Auto reply**: off by default. When on, the bot answers by itself in the channels of the group, only when it is confident enough. The _**Minimum confidence**_ slider goes from 80% to 95%.
* **Questions and answers**: _**+ Add a question**_. The answer is sent exactly as you wrote it (up to 200 characters for a question and 1800 for an answer).
