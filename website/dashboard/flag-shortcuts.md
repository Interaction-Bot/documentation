# Flag shortcuts

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dash-flag-shortcuts.png" alt="Flag shortcuts page"><figcaption><p>Flag shortcuts page</p></figcaption></figure>

## Localization

Navbar > category _**Management**_ > _**Flag shortcuts**_.

The bot adds the chosen flags under every message of the channel, so a member only has to click a flag to get the translation. Between 1 and 5 flags per channel. The same can be done with [`/flags`](../../commands/flags.md).

Click _**Add a channel**_, choose the text channel, then pick the flags (searchable list of countries and languages). _**Remove this channel**_ stops the shortcuts. Then click _**Save**_.
