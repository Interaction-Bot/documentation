# November 2026

The November update reorganizes the [dashboard](../website/dashboard/README.md) in five sections, replaces the configuration commands with the dashboard and the AI setup, and adds new features to manage your server. Limits are given as **Free / Premium**, see [Premium](../premium/premium.md).

## 27 November 2026: the first day of a new server

* **No vote needed during the first 24 hours** after the bot is added to a server: text to speech, speech to text, transcription and recording work right away. See [Voice: Access](../audio/voice.md#access).
* **Translation from the first minute**: a new server gets a **Whole server** rule into its language. See [Translation rules](../website/dashboard/translation-rules.md#the-rule-of-a-new-server).
* **Voices of the members on by default**: members can choose their voice with `/tts voice`, and each member gets a voice of their own in a `/tts start` session. A server that saved its text to speech settings keeps them.
* The private reminder to vote that followed a command once a day is no longer sent.
* **The vote is personal**: `/tts say`, `/tts start`, `/transcribe start`, `/transcribe live`, `/record start` and the transcription of voice messages need **your own** top.gg vote (or a Premium server). The vote of another member no longer unlocks the whole server. In a TTS channel, the messages of members who voted are read; an automatic transcription starts when someone in the voice channel voted. See [Voice: Access](../audio/voice.md#access).
* **`/connect`** needs no vote and no Premium any more.
* **`/join` is now `/tempvoice join`** (access to a temporary voice channel with its password): `/join` was often taken for the command that brings the bot into a voice channel, which is `/connect`. `/tempvoice` is now visible to every member; its administration commands still need *Manage Server*.

## 22 November 2026: the big update

### Dashboard menu

The menu has been regrouped, and each feature has one page:

* **Overview**, **Quick setup**, **Journal** and **Premium**.
* **Translation**: **Translation Rules**, **Flags**, **Exceptions** and **Voice messages**.
* **Audio & AI**: **Transcription**, **Text to speech**, **FAQ**, **Voice recording** and **Temp voice**.
* **Management**: **Welcome & goodbye**, **Invitations**, **Honeypot**, **Reaction roles**, **Bot profile** and **Event log**.

The former pages (channel, role, category and mirror translation, flag shortcuts, minimum message size, blacklist) are now part of these pages. A disabled feature stays in the menu, greyed out, and keeps its settings.

### Translation rules

* The **Translation Rules** page holds all the rules of the server: channels, categories, two languages in one channel, linked channels, the whole server, and roles. See [Translation rules](../website/dashboard/translation-rules.md).
* Limits: **30 rules** (Free) or **200** (Premium), and **5 languages per rule** (Free) or **20** (Premium).
* The **Flags** page has two cards: **React with a flag**, and **Automatically added flags**.
* The **Exceptions** page holds the channels, roles and languages that are never translated, and the minimum size of a message.
* A message translated by a Premium server can be translated into up to 20 languages; a free server, up to 3.

### Text to speech and transcription rules

* Text to speech and transcription are set by **rules**: for a channel, for a category, or for the whole server. The most precise rule applies first.
* Text to speech: **5 rules** (Free) or **25** (Premium). Transcription: **1 rule** (Free) or **25** (Premium), with up to 3 destinations per voice channel on Premium.
* A rule for the whole server can make the bot join the voice channel of the person who writes. An option limits it to the members whose microphone is off.

### Language detection

To detect the language of a short message, the bot now looks at the previous messages of the same author: in this channel first, then elsewhere on the server. The setting **Messages used to detect the language** (from 1 to 10, default 5) is on the [Overview](../website/dashboard/overview.md) page.

### Welcome and goodbye

* A **welcome hub** channel holds the rules of the server: there is no longer a separate text of rules to write.
* The **message builder** writes welcome, goodbye, banner and temporary voice messages, with several blocks, images, and a JSON or Discohook import. See [Welcome and goodbye](../management/welcome-and-goodbye.md).
* Automatic roles are given to new members.

### Reaction roles (new)

The **Reaction roles** page lets members pick their roles with emojis under a message, with buttons or with a dropdown menu. The panels are free. See [Reaction roles](../website/dashboard/reaction-roles.md).

### Invitations

* The invitation tracker is on by default when the bot joins a server.
* The **pinned leaderboards** of the invitations are free.
* Invitations can be read by group, and the page shows the fake accounts and the reasons of each verdict. See [Invite tracker](../management/invite-tracker.md).

### Bot profile (Premium)

The **Bot profile** page sets the nickname, avatar, banner and bio of the bot on your server only. It needs Premium. See [Bot profile](../website/dashboard/bot-profile.md).

### Quick setup (new)

The **Quick setup** page guides you through the first settings of the bot, in one place.

### Overview

* The **Bot settings** card has an **Announcements channel**: the channel where the bot posts its own messages.
* The **Your feedback** section lets you give a review, report a problem, suggest an idea or vote on top.gg.
* The **AI assistant** card opens the assistant from the Overview page.

### Journal

The **Journal** page lists the actions made on the bot on your server (with who, when, from where, and what changed), and the problems the bot met. It is kept 90 days by default. See [Journal](../website/dashboard/journal.md).

### Account and privacy

* You can opt out of the recording of your voice, and of the processing of your messages, from the **Privacy** card of the [Account page](../website/dashboard/account.md).
* **Download my data** and **Delete my data** are on the same card.
* Cloned voices are for members aged **18 or over**. Each voice is stored encrypted, and it is deleted 30 days after the end of the Premium of its owner.

### Premium

* Premium is bought on the [Premium page](https://interaction-bot.com/premium) with Stripe, with one monthly subscription: **1 server €3.99**, **3 servers €9.99** or **10 servers €19.99** per month. You can change plan from **My subscription**. See [Subscribe](../premium/subscribe.md).
* The translation of the messages of bots and the blacklist are Premium options of a server.
* The Premium comparison table is on the [Premium page](../premium/premium.md).

### Invite link

The invitation link of the bot asks for new permissions: voice messages, voice channel status, mute members, deafen members, priority speaker and soundboard. The link does not ask for **Kick** or **Ban** members: the honeypot asks for them when it is set up.

### Commands

* The bot no longer has configuration commands. Set up the bot on the dashboard, or with `/ai setup`. The command `/config` says how.
* `/help` opens a card with the sections of the bot, in the order of the dashboard. See [All commands](../commands/all-commands.md).

### Status page

* A **Platform** section lists the website, the dashboard, the bot link and the translation services.
* Each part has a history of its last 7 days, with its uptime.
* New states: **Update in progress** and **Restarting**. The clusters of the bot are restarted once a day, one after the other, so a short restart can show on the page. See [Status](../website/status.md).

## 7 November 2026

* **Text to speech**: the dashboard has new options: read a mention as a word, expand the abbreviations, and a voice for each member (off by default).
* **Activity**: the live transcription can be launched from a text channel: the bot uses the voice channel of the member who launches it. When the bot is moved to another channel, its message tells which command to use.
