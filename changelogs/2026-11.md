# November 2026

The update that brings the new translation rules to the [dashboard](../website/dashboard/README.md), and new pages to follow what the bot does on your server. Limits are given as **Free / Premium**, see [Premium](../premium/premium.md).

## New features

### Translation rules
* The **Translation rules** page regroups the rules of your server in one place: **Channels**, **Categories**, **Two languages in one channel**, **Linked channels**, **Whole server** and **Roles**.
* Opening the page converts your server to the rules engine, once. The former pages (Channel, Role, Category and Mirror Translation) are no longer in the menu.
* **Try a sentence** shows how the rules would translate a sentence (300 characters at most), and **Check a channel** shows which rules apply to a channel.
* The rules can also be managed in Discord with `/translation`, see [Translation commands](../commands/translation.md).
* Limits: **30 rules** (Free) / **200 rules** (Premium), **5 languages per rule** (Free) / **20** (Premium).
* See [Channel Translation](../website/dashboard/channel-translation.md).

### Translation menu
* **Flags**: the page has two cards, **React with a flag** and **Automatically added flags**.
* **Exceptions**: channels, roles and languages that are never translated, and the minimum message size.
* **Voice messages**: the voice message settings of the server.

### Journal
* A new **Journal** page in the menu lists the changes made to the bot on your server, and the problems the bot met. See [Journal](../website/dashboard/journal.md).

### Overview
* The **Bot settings** card has an **Announcements channel**: the channel where the bot posts its own messages.
* A **Premium** or **Free** badge at the top of the page shows the plan of your server.

### Premium
* Premium can be subscribed to on the website, with Stripe. See [Premium](../premium/premium.md).

### Status page
* A **Platform** section lists the translation services, the Website, the Dashboard and the Bot link.
* The clusters have a history with an uptime percentage, a **Recent changes** section and a banner for the global state.
* New states: **Update in progress** and **Restarting**.
* See [Status](../website/status.md).
