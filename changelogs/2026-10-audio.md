# October 2026 - audio update

The second big update of October 2026. It adds the audio suite, redesigns the [dashboard](../website/dashboard/README.md) and adds personal pages on the website. Limits are given as **Free / Premium**, see [Premium](../premium/premium.md).

## New dashboard design

* A **sidebar** with a **server picker**, sections you can fold (_Translation_, _Audio & AI_, _Management_) and icons for each page. On a phone, it opens with the burger button.
* **Category pages**: click a section name to see its extensions as cards, each with a switch, a status and a _Configure_ button.
* **Every feature is an extension** that can be turned on or off per server, **without losing its settings**. A disabled extension is greyed out in the sidebar and its page offers an _Enable_ button. One _Save_ applies all your changes.
* The dashboard warns when the bot is **missing a permission** for an extension, and gives a button to reinvite it with the right permissions.
* The website can be read in the language of your choice (user menu), and the pages work on a phone.
* See [Dashboard](../website/dashboard/README.md).

## Voice recording

* `/record start` records the voice channel you are in, with **one track per speaker**, an announcement in the text channel and a panel with a _Stop recording_ button. `/record stop`, `/record note`, `/record list`, `/record test`, `/record transcribe` and `/record auto on|off|view` complete it.
* Recordings are stored **encrypted** and kept **24 hours (Free) / 7 days (Premium)**. Listen to them, download each track or everything at once on the new [My recordings](../website/recordings.md) page.
* New dashboard page [Voice recording](../website/dashboard/voice-recording.md).
* See [Voice recording](../audio/recording.md).

## Temporary voice channels

* A **creator channel** ("Join to create"): members who join it get their own voice channel, deleted when it is empty. `/tempvoice setup` installs it in one command.
* The owner controls the channel with `/voice …` commands or with a **panel of buttons**: name, user limit, privacy, waiting room, password, invitations, trusted and blocked members, kick and vote kick, region, bitrate, claim and transfer.
* A moderation log, roles allowed to create channels and the vote share are set with `/tempvoice config` or on the dashboard page [Temp voice](../website/dashboard/temp-voice.md).
* Temporary voice channels need no vote and no premium server.
* See [Temporary voice channels](../audio/tempvoice.md).

## Live transcription (Activity)

* `/transcribe live` opens a Discord **Activity** that shows the transcription live, line by line, with the picture of each speaker, using the transcription already running (no extra cost).
* On a premium server, each viewer can read it **in the language of their choice**.
* A recording can be started from the Activity, and the transcription can be stopped with a confirmation.
* Settings on the [Speech to text](../website/dashboard/speech-to-text.md) page.
* `/transcribe auto manage` manages the automatic transcription with a panel.
* See [Live transcription (Activity)](../audio/live-transcription.md).

## Voices and voice cloning

* `/tts voice` chooses the voice that reads your messages. `/tts say`, `/tts start`, `/tts stop` and `/tts settings` replace the previous `/tts` and `/tts-stop`, and the TTS channels have a management panel (`/tts-channel manage`).
* **Voice cloning** (Premium): `/tts clone create|list|delete` and the website page [My voice](../website/my-voice.md) create a voice from a recording of **your own** voice, with an explicit consent. You can delete it at any time.
* See [Voices and voice cloning](../audio/voices-and-cloning.md).

## Limits and privacy

* `/limits` shows the daily audio time of the server, with a warning at 80 %. The limits are **per server and per UTC day**: transcription and text to speech **30 minutes / 3 hours**, recording **1 h 45 / 6 hours**.
* `/privacy` and the **Privacy** card of the [Account](../website/dashboard/account.md) page show what the bot keeps about you. You can **opt out** of the processing of your messages and of the recording of your voice, **download** your data, **delete** your data or your account.
* See [Limits and privacy](../audio/limits-and-privacy.md).

## Website

* New [Commands page](../website/commands.md) with search, filters and one-click copy.
* New [feature pages](../website/features.md) with limits by plan.
* New pages [My recordings](../website/recordings.md) and [My voice](../website/my-voice.md).

## Corrections

* The transcription limit of a premium server is **3 hours per UTC day** (the documentation said 2 hours over 24 hours from the first transcription).
* `/reminder enable` failed on first use. The vote check no longer fails when the database has an error, and top.gg statistics errors are no longer ignored.
* Transcription: a double click no longer starts two sessions, the bot no longer stays alone in the channel when a forum cannot be opened, and `/transcribe start` respects the channel where the bot already is.
