# Limits and privacy

Three commands explain the limits and your data:

| Command | What it does | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`/limits`](#daily-limits) | Shows the daily audio limits of the server: used, left and when they start again. | Everybody |
| [`/reminder`](#vote-reminder-reminder) | Reminds you to vote for the bot on top.gg. | Everybody |
| [`/privacy`](#what-the-bot-keeps-privacy) | Shows what the bot keeps about you and the state of your opt-outs. | Everybody |

The opt-outs themselves are set on the **Account** page of the website, see [Privacy opt-outs](#privacy-opt-outs).

## Daily limits

The audio features have a daily limit **per server**. The usage of all the channels and sessions of the server adds up, and every limit starts again every day at **midnight UTC**.

| Kind | What is counted | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transcription | The time of the transcription sessions: `/transcribe start`, automatic transcription and the [live transcription](live-transcription.md). | 30 minutes | 3 hours |
| Text to speech | The duration of the speech generated by `/tts` and the TTS channels. | 30 minutes | 3 hours |
| Recording | The duration of the recordings (`/record`, also started from the Activity). | 1 hour 45 minutes | 6 hours |

### What happens at the limit

* **80 %**: the server is warned once a day. Example: *This server has used 80% of its daily Transcription time: 6 min left. It resets in 5 hours.*
* **100 %**: the limit is reached. The notice says when it starts again.
  * A **transcription** stops, and the bot leaves the voice channel. `/transcribe start` and the automatic transcription are refused until the reset.
  * **Text to speech** stops, and the bot leaves the voice channel.
  * A **recording** stops.
* On a **free** server, the notices come with a button to the [premium page](../premium/premium.md). A server that becomes premium is measured against the premium limit at once, the same day.

### `/limits`

Run `/limits` to see the limits of your server. The answer is private (only you see it) and shows, for each kind:

* a gauge of ten squares and the percentage used: green, then amber from 80 %, then red at the limit (the percentage is always written, not only shown by colour);
* the time used, the limit, the time left and the reset time (for example *in 5 hours*).

It also tells whether the server has premium. On a free server, a button leads to the premium page.

{% hint style="info" %}
The limits are the same for every server of the bot, and they are not configurable from the dashboard.
{% endhint %}

### Who can use the features

The daily limits are only one part of the rule. A feature also needs a premium server or a vote on top.gg, and the rule depends on the feature. Details are in [Voice: Access](voice.md#access).

| Feature | Premium server | Vote on top.gg (valid 24 hours) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/tts`, `/connect` | No, the vote is still required | The member who runs the command |
| `/transcribe start`, `/transcribe live` | Yes | The member who runs the command |
| TTS channels, automatic transcription | Yes | A vote of any member of the server, for the whole server |
| Start of the live transcription from the Activity | Yes | A vote of any member of the server |
| Recording (`/record`) | Yes | A vote of any member of the server |

## Vote reminder (`/reminder`)

`/reminder enable:True` turns the reminder on, `/reminder enable:False` turns it off. The same switch is on the **Account** page.

* While the reminder is on, the bot sends you a **private message** once a day, as long as you have not voted for the bot in the last 24 hours. The message contains the link to the [top.gg vote page](https://top.gg/bot/893494390142697493/vote).
* The bot checks the reminders every hour, so a message can come up to an hour after the 24 hours have passed.
* If you have closed your private messages, the reminder is not sent.
* If the reminder is already on (or off), the bot answers *The reminder is already enabled* (or *disabled*).

## What the bot keeps (`/privacy`)

`/privacy` shows the data kept about you, your two opt-outs, and a link to the Account page. The text of the command is:

| Data | How long it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your **voice** in the recordings (`/record`) where you speak | 24 hours. 7 days on a premium server. |
| Your **notes** in the recordings | As long as the recording that holds them. |
| Your **settings** (language, notifications, voice, preferences) | Until you change them or delete your account. |

The rest of what the bot handles:

* **Transcriptions** (`/transcribe`, live transcription): the text is posted in the text channel of the transcription. The bot does not store it in its database. The lines of the live transcription are kept in memory while the transcription runs only (see [Live transcription: Privacy](live-transcription.md#privacy)).
* **Usage limits**: the bot keeps the daily time used by each server (for [`/limits`](#daily-limits)). These counts are per server, not per member.
* **Message translations** follow the [message opt-out](#privacy-opt-outs) below.

On the **Account** page you can also download a copy of your data (a ZIP file), delete your data (your voice in the recordings, the recordings you started and your settings), or delete your account. See [Account: Privacy](../website/dashboard/account.md#privacy).

## Privacy opt-outs

Two opt-outs are on the **Account** page, in the **Privacy** card (`interaction-bot.com/account`). `/privacy` shows their current state.

| Opt-out | What it does | What it does not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Do not record me** | From the next recording, the bot does not record your voice. | It does not change the recordings already made. Use **Delete my data** on the Account page for those. |
| **Don't process my messages** | The bot no longer translates, detects or reads aloud your **new** messages, and refuses to translate them. | It does not delete the messages already processed, and it does not block your own commands. |

Both changes apply when you save them on the Account page. Your choice is read again at once.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The **Do not record me** opt-out covers **recordings** only. Speech in a voice channel that is transcribed (`/transcribe start`, automatic transcription or the live transcription) is transcribed as usual, even if you opted out. To stop a transcription of the channel, a member with *Manage Server* can run `/transcribe stop`. You can also avoid speaking in the channel while the transcription runs.
{% endhint %}

If the bot cannot read your choice at the moment (a temporary database problem):

* for **recordings**, nothing of yours is recorded until the choice can be read again, to be safe;
* for **messages**, your messages keep being processed as usual.
