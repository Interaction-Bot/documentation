# Q&A overview

Moderators write a **FAQ** for the server: questions and answers, organized in **groups**. Members get the answers either on demand with `/ask`, or automatically when they ask a question in a channel linked to a group. The FAQ is written and organized on the [FAQ page of the dashboard](../website/dashboard/faq.md). The bot never generates an answer: it only sends the answers your moderators wrote, translated into the language of the question when needed.

## Choose your method

| I want to...                                                       | Use                                                              | Needs the dashboard |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Let members search the FAQ when they want                          | [`/ask`](faq.md#ask)                                             | No (the group is chosen in the dashboard) |
| Let the bot answer questions by itself in some channels            | [Automatic replies](faq.md#automatic-replies)                    | Yes                 |
| Add, edit or remove questions and answers                          | [FAQ dashboard page](../website/dashboard/faq.md)                | Yes                 |
| Organize questions in groups and link channels to them             | [FAQ dashboard page](../website/dashboard/faq.md)                | Yes                 |

There is no `/faq` command: the FAQ is managed only on the dashboard.

## Good to know

* Free servers have 1 group, premium servers 5. See the [limits](faq.md#limits).
* Answers are translated, so one FAQ serves members of every language.
* A member who gets a wrong automatic answer can click **Not helpful** to remove it.
